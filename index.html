  • XChannel: your packet can follow the advertisements to the Krispy Kreme, but to come home with a donut it needs to leave a channel across every bridge. XChannel contains a channelId for bridges to rewrite and a channel control word (SYN, FIN).
  • XBlob: Your computer local data, crossing system and network boundaries. XBlob is X bus local web blobs that the X bus knows how to move between systems and bridges know how to move between networks.
  • XIntent: Send your ui.TextToSpeech intents from your phone to your desktop (across a bridge over tailscale). Demand a higher quality of speech synthesis.
  • XAudio: Remember wanting audio to follow ssh -X? Send your sound drops to any system in your network thats advertised as `[XAudioSink] name="cool-speakers"`
  • XHtml: Remember all those times you were promised that you would write an html card and it would have access to the intent commands on your entire network?
